3. Defining Pressure: The Multi-Dimensional Cricket Pressure Index (CPI)

Pressure in cricket is continuous, dynamic, and structurally asymmetric between innings. We model pressure as a continuous index CPI ∈ [0, 1] for every single delivery in the dataset.

A. Innings 2 (Chasing Situations): Win Probability Leverage

In a chase, every ball alters the probability of winning. We trained a HistGradientBoostingClassifier (120 trees, max depth 31, learning rate 0.08) on 95,146 second-innings deliveries using features: Runs Needed, Balls Remaining, Wickets in Hand, Required Run Rate (RRR), Current Run Rate (CRR), Target, and Knockout Stage flag.

Win Probability Uncertainty: U = 4 × P(Win) × (1 - P(Win)) ∈ [0, 1] Leverage Index: LI = U × [0.4 + 0.6 × (120 - BallsRemaining) / 120] Chase Stress: S_chase = 0.50 × Stress(RRR) + 0.30 × Stress(Wickets) + 0.20 × Stress(LateGame) CPI_inn2 = clip(0.50 × LI + 0.40 × S_chase + 0.10 × IsKnockout, 0, 1)

B. Innings 1 (Target Setting): Crisis Rebuilding & Death Acceleration

Early Collapse Stress: S_collapse = clip([WicketsLost - (OverID × 0.35 + 0.5)] / 3.0, 0, 1) Death Phase Stress: S_death = clip([(OverID - 15) / 5.0] × (0.8 + 0.05 × WicketsLost), 0, 1) CPI_inn1 = clip(0.45 × S_death + 0.35 × S_collapse + 0.10 × ParDeficit + 0.10 × IsKnockout, 0, 1) Super Overs: CPI = 1.0 (Maximum Stress)
Classification Cutoff
Deliveries with CPI ≥ 0.28 represent the top 22.7% most critical match situations (45,684 balls), providing robust statistical sample sizes while sharply filtering out cruise-control deliveries.

4. Performance Measurement: Format-Calibrated Contextual Par (FCCP)

Solving the Baseline Distortion Paradox
A naive model comparing a player's pressure stats to their own overall stats penalizes world-class death bowlers like Jasprit Bumrah. In low-leverage overs, typical league economy is ~7.5 RPO; in death overs, league economy is 10.4 RPO. When Bumrah bowls death overs at 7.49 RPO, comparing him to his own 6.61 baseline makes him look like he "declined" (-0.88), when in reality he saved +2.14 runs per over relative to situational par!

To ensure complete scientific fairness across competitions, we benchmark every delivery against its Format-Calibrated Contextual Par (FCCP), computed by (Is_IPL, Innings, Over_ID, Is_High_Pressure):

  • IPL vs T20I Disparity: IPL high-pressure situations average 9.95 RPO and 148.6 SR, whereas T20Is average 9.08 RPO and 135.2 SR. FCCP prevents false biases between international and franchise matches.
  • Batting Runs Added: SR_Lift = Observed_SR - Format_Par_SR.
  • Bowling Runs Saved: Runs_Saved_Per_Over = Format_Par_Econ - Observed_Econ.
  • Bayesian Shrinkage: All rates regularized toward league prior means with prior weight K = 50 balls: Shrunk_Metric = (Balls × Observed + K × LeaguePar) / (Balls + K).

5. Reliability, Consistency & Limitations

  • Match-Level Variance Testing: We calculated the standard deviation of each player's value-added across individual matches. Players with high volatility were penalized, while players with steady match-to-match clutch delivery (Buttler, Bumrah, Surya, Pathirana, Shashank) were rewarded.
  • Sample Eligibility Gates: Strict thresholds of ≥ 100 high-pressure balls and ≥ 200 baseline balls prevent small-sample distortions while ensuring multi-season consistency.
